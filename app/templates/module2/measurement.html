Step 2 — Real-world 2D measurement

Upload an image, enter the object distance Z, then click two points. The clicks are converted from canvas coordinates to full-resolution image coordinates server-side, undistorted with cv2.undistortPoints, and backprojected to 3D at depth Z. The dimension is the distance between the two backprojected points.

Getting Z right matters more than anything else here. Measure to the camera's optical centre, not the phone's back glass — the difference is 5–10 mm, a systematic bias on every measurement. Keep the object centred in frame, or the straight-line distance is Z/cos θ rather than Z. Measure to the face being measured.
