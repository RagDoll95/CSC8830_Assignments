The method in one line

u = f_x · X/Z + c_x v = f_y · Y/Z + c_y (forward projection) X = (u − c_x) · Z / f_x Y = (v − c_y) · Z / f_y (inverted at known Z)

Backprojecting a pixel to a 3D point at known depth is the only primitive needed: a dimension is the Euclidean distance between two backprojections, so width, height and diagonal all come from one function. For an axis-aligned span it collapses to W = Δu · Z / f_x.
