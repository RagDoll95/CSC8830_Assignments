Why Zhang's method rather than a DLT fit

A direct linear transform on a single calibration object recovers a projection matrix, but it does not model radial distortion, cannot impose constraints such as a known focal length or zero skew, and minimises an algebraic residual rather than the geometrically meaningful reprojection error. Zhang's method observes one planar target in many orientations, which both supplies the constraints needed to separate the intrinsics from the per-view extrinsics and lets the distortion coefficients be estimated — then refines everything by nonlinear least squares on reprojection error, which is the number reported above.
