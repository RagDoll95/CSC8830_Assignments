Method Zhang, and why not a DLT fit

Corners are refined to subpixel accuracy with cornerSubPix and fitted with cv2.calibrateCamera, OpenCV's implementation of Zhang's multi-plane method.

A direct linear transform on a single calibration object recovers a projection matrix, but it does not model radial distortion, cannot impose constraints such as a known focal length or zero skew, and minimises an algebraic residual rather than the geometrically meaningful reprojection error. Zhang's method observes one planar target in many orientations, which both supplies the constraints needed to separate the intrinsics from the per-view extrinsics and lets the distortion coefficients be estimated — then refines everything by nonlinear least squares on reprojection error, which is the RMS reported above.

K is in pixels, so it is only valid at the resolution it was fitted at: every image must share one resolution, and measurements must use that same resolution.
