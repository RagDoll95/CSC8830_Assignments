Limitations

The whole method rests on one assumption: the measured face is fronto-parallel and at a single depth Z. Any tilt of the object plane, or any thickness between the front edge and the face being measured, violates it — the projected span then corresponds to a range of depths rather than one, and no single Z makes the computation exact. A single view cannot recover depth, which is why Z has to be measured with a tape at all; the two-camera derivation shows how a second view removes that dependence via triangulation.
