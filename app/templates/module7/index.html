Use the same calibrated camera at two positions. Move it to the right by a measured baseline, keeping its orientation, height, lens, focus and zoom fixed. The scene must stay still. Use a straight rail or a rigid guide; a handheld pan does not satisfy this model.

{% if room %}

A phone camera cannot see a whole room at once: from inside a rectangular room, the two corners next to any corner are at least 90° apart. So measure each edge separately. In one video, face a wall, record a stationary view, slide the camera right by the baseline, record a second stationary view, then turn to the next wall and repeat. For each edge, choose the timestamps of the two stops that face it. Disable digital stabilization and calibrate in the actual video mode.

{% endif %}
